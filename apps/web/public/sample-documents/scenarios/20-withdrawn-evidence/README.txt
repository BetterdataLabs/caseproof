CASEPROOF | A RECEIPT IS WITHDRAWN AFTER COMPLETION

Reopen only affected work and retain the superseded summary.

Seller: Orchid Bay Garments Pte Ltd
Buyer: Lionstar Retail Pte Ltd
Invoice: INV-303
Flow: withdrawal, summary_supersession, permitted_logistics_issuer

SETUP
Use New case > Demo scenarios > Uniform order - invoice INV-303, in Autonomous mode. Its buyer-authorised logistics provider can issue receipt evidence in this prepared case. Ordinary uploads require a buyer-issued receipt.

START WITH
01-inv-303-v1.pdf
02-r1-303.pdf

EXPECTED FIRST RESULT
A valid invoice and authorised logistics receipt initially complete. Withdrawal must invalidate the delivery result.

STEPS
1. Create INV-303 and wait for Review complete. Download its summary.
2. In Customer exchange (demo), send Seller withdraws the receipt.
3. Confirm the case reopens: the invoice check still passes, receipt coverage is missing, and the earlier summary is Superseded.
4. Send Reissued receipt for all 50 cartons from the logistics provider. Wait for a new completed summary.

EXPECTED FINAL RESULT
Document review completes with a retained summary.
