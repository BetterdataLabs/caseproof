CASEPROOF | REMINDER, ESCALATION, THEN EVIDENCE

Waiting consumes no model calls; a reviewer can authorise another request.

Seller: Jurong Fresh Produce Pte Ltd
Buyer: East Coast Hospitality Group Pte Ltd
Invoice: INV-505
Flow: reminder, no_response, extra_request_round

SETUP
Use New case > Demo scenarios > Fresh produce - invoice INV-505, in Autonomous mode. The included documents match that prepared case. The demo clock is available only on prepared cases.

START WITH
01-inv-505-v1.pdf

EXPECTED FIRST RESULT
An invoice with no receipt waits for the business. Under the default policy, 48 hours triggers a reminder and another 72 hours triggers human escalation.

STEPS
1. Create the INV-505 demo case and wait for the missing-receipt request.
2. In Customer exchange (demo), advance the demo clock by 48 hours. Verify one reminder appears without a new model call.
3. Advance another 72 hours. Open the No reply decision and authorise a further request round, explaining that the business needs one more chance to reply.
4. In Customer exchange (demo), send Receipt R1 for all 240 crates. Wait for completion.

EXPECTED FINAL RESULT
Document review completes with a retained summary.
