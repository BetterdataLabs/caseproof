CASEPROOF | A SUPPLIER CHANGES ITS INVOICE FORM

A template mismatch is a review question, not proof of fraud.

Seller: Alder Trade Supplies Pte Ltd
Buyer: Cedar Commercial Services Pte Ltd
Invoice: AT-2026-1501
Flow: template_mismatch, appearance_acceptance

SETUP
In Optional document checks, supply reference-invoice.pdf as the Invoice reference PDF. Leave tampering unchecked to isolate template comparison.

START WITH
01-invoice.pdf
02-full-receipt.pdf

EXPECTED FIRST RESULT
The business values agree, but the supplied invoice uses a different header and table layout from the reference.

STEPS
1. Upload 01 as the invoice and 02 as the receipt, selecting reference-invoice.pdf only in the invoice reference field.
2. Open the document appearance task. Compare the highlighted areas with Show reference template.
3. For this fictional test, record: The issuer has confirmed its new form; transaction details are unchanged. Choose Record appearance review and continue.
4. Both the reference mismatch and the recorded human acceptance should remain in the summary.

EXPECTED FINAL RESULT
Document review completes with a retained summary.

FILES
01-invoice.pdf: invoice
02-full-receipt.pdf: receipt
reference-invoice.pdf: invoice_template

All companies, people, addresses and transactions are fictional.
The business PDFs are test records, not genuine invoices or evidence of real deliveries.
