# Public-company research demonstration

These two PDFs contain a **synthetic transaction**, explicitly labelled as demonstration data. They are not issued by, authorised by, or sent to Singapore Airlines Limited. The company is used only as an identifiable subject for live public-source research.

1. Create a case with `singapore-airlines-demo-invoice.pdf` and `singapore-airlines-demo-receipt.pdf`.
2. Enable **Include company credit research**, choose **Buyer**, and enter **Singapore** as the country of registration. Leave the optional registration number empty.
3. Open **Research** and select the new case. The agent saves three searches and synthesises their cited passages into six research areas.

Research adds USD 1.00 to the normal case budget. The public findings come from live Google-grounded searches; the PDFs contain no financial analysis or prewritten research result.
